A few useful answers

Before we connect.

Can you work with our existing network?

Yes. A review of your current setup helps identify what is working, what needs attention and where a targeted change could help. A complete replacement is not always necessary.

Can you help with unreliable business Wi-Fi?

Wireless coverage depends on the building, access point placement, interference and the devices using it. A structured assessment can identify the cause and guide improvements.

What should we prepare for a network project?

Start with your sites, approximate user and device numbers, essential applications, current issues and any planned changes. Existing network diagrams and equipment lists are useful if you have them.
